About JoCare Pharmacy

Patient-Centered Care Rooted in Our Community

Dedicated to safe medication dispensing, clear health education, and accessible digital tools for families and seniors in Maramag, Bukidnon.

Our Mission

Bridging Trusted Local Care with Digital Convenience

JoCare Pharmacy was founded to ensure local residents never have to choose between personal pharmacist guidance and modern convenience. We eliminate long counter queues by letting patients submit refills and book health screenings online ahead of time.

Every prescription is carefully verified for drug interactions, clear dosage labeling, and senior-friendly instructions before leaving our counter.

Our Team of Licensed Pharmacists

Experienced healthcare professionals committed to accurate dispensing and compassionate consultation.

Safety Standards & Community Commitment

Guided by recognized pharmacy practice, patient privacy, and web accessibility standards.

Double-Check Verification

Every prescription undergoes a two-step pharmacist accuracy and interaction check prior to release.

Confidential Records

Patient refill submissions and health questions are handled with strict confidentiality and data security.

Senior & PWD Priority

High-contrast readable labels, priority express counter pickup, and scheduled home delivery support.

Community Health Drives

Monthly free blood pressure monitoring and barangay wellness education workshops in Maramag.
